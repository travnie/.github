The shared workshop hosts multiple plugins. The **rolling Latest** release contains separate assets; do not assume installing one bundle installs the others.

| Project | What it does | Maintained source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paint.NET ICO | Multi-image ICO import/export for Paint.NET 5.1 and 5.2+ | [ICO](https://github.com/travnie/twojstar/tree/main/addons/paintdotnet/ico) |
| Paint.NET AI | Local Restore, DeJPEG, and Denoise effects | [AI effects](https://github.com/travnie/twojstar/tree/main/addons/paintdotnet/ai) |
| Audacity Auto Declip | Local-first audio restoration VST3, Windows and Linux builds | [Audacity](https://github.com/travnie/twojstar/tree/main/addons/audacity) |
