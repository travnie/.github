## One rolling release

[Latest](https://github.com/travnie/twojstar/releases/latest) is the **repository-wide** bundle snapshot, not a separate version train per tool. Product CI builds relevant artifacts, and a shared publisher assembles the release before advancing Latest. Experiments such as Intent Keyboard are not automatically downloadable products.
